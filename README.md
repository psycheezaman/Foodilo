## Food Wastage Reduction Android Apps (Foodilo)
**"Foodilo"** is an android application to accelerate and support community-based initiatives in reducing domestic food waste. Food Wastage Reduction Android Apps (Foodilo) combat food waste by empowering users with intuitive tools to manage inventory, plan meals efficiently, and donate surplus food. The donator can create the account and whenever they are having wastage food they can login and give request to the admin. Foodilo demands putting up details of the leftover food along with Pickup address and other relevant details which can be viewed by the Receivers. Foodilo serve as catalysts for societal change, fostering a culture of compassion and environmental sustainable management of natural resources and ecosystems.

## Aims
This project provide a straightforward interface that encourages centered around communities approaches to reduce food waste. Connects local receivers with donors. Raises awareness of the need to decrease food waste. Moreover, makes it easier for unused food to be contributed.
